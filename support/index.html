Support

Write to support@8086.llc. A person reads every message. We answer what you send; nothing else is collected.

Common questions

Who is Talktitles for?

Talktitles is for two people in the same room, one phone lying between you, each reading the other's speech in your own language. It is not for phone calls, not for groups of three or more, and not for translating text or photos.

How do I start?

Choose the language you speak and the language the other person speaks, then tap Start. Lay the phone flat between you. The half nearer the other person is turned to face them.

It says a language isn't on this phone yet.

Talktitles translates on the phone, so it needs each language downloaded once. Tap Download while you have Wi-Fi or mobile data. After that, it works with no connection.

Does it work with no connection?

Yes, once both languages are downloaded. Your conversation is never uploaded. The phone uses the network only to buy or restore the unlock and to install a language pack (and, on Android, for the basic diagnostics Google's translation library sends).

A line was misheard. What do I do?

Tap the finished line and change what was heard. The translation updates on both halves and the line is marked "Fixed". If the line landed on the wrong side, tap "Move to the other side", or tap "That was me" on your half.

How do I keep the conversation?

Tap Pause, then Share transcript. You get a text file with both languages in order. Nothing is saved unless you share it.

Talktitles can't hear us.

Turn on the microphone for Talktitles in your phone's Settings, then come back. If another app takes the microphone, Talktitles pauses. Tap Resume when it is free again.

How much does it cost?

The download is free and includes 20 conversation minutes. Time counts only while Talktitles is listening. One purchase, $9.99 in the U.S. (the store shows your local price), unlocks unlimited conversation time. There is no subscription. Reading, fixing and sharing transcripts stay free.

I bought it on another phone, or reinstalled.

Tap Restore purchase on the unlock screen or in About, signed in to the same Apple Account or Google account. On Android, deleting the app also resets the free minutes. The purchase is restored from Google Play.

How do I get a refund?

Purchases go through the store. App Store: Request a refund for apps or content. Google Play: Refunds on Google Play.

Can I rely on the translation?

Captions are machine translations and can be wrong. Do not use them for medical, legal or emergency conversations.
