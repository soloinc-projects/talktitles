Privacy policy

Talktitles, by 8086 LLC. Effective 25 October 2026.

In short: your conversation never leaves the phone, and we collect nothing. There is no account, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics and no crash reporting, and we run no server. On iPhone and iPad the App Store label reads "Data Not Collected". On Android, Google's on-device translation library (ML Kit) sends Google basic diagnostics, so the Google Play label lists them; see The network.

Your conversation

Speech recognition and translation run on your phone. Conversation audio is used only to make captions and is not recorded. Caption text is kept in the phone's memory for that conversation only and is gone when it ends. It is never shown in a notification or on the Lock Screen: those show only the two languages and whether Talktitles is listening.

A transcript exists only if you choose Share transcript. Talktitles writes a temporary text file for the system share sheet and removes it from the phone when the share sheet closes. Where it goes from there is your choice.

The network

Your conversation is never uploaded. On iPhone and iPad the only time the phone uses the network is when you buy or restore the unlock, or when you install a language pack. On Android it also uses it for the basic diagnostics Google's translation library sends. The purchase goes through the App Store or Google Play. A language download comes from Apple (iPhone and iPad) or from Google's on-device translation models (Android). None of these carries any of your conversation. The app's own code contains no networking.

On Android, Google's ML Kit translation library sends Google the device's make, model and OS version, the app's name and version, an identifier for this installation, performance and error codes, and the two languages you chose, as Google describes in its ML Kit data disclosure. It never sends caption text or audio. We do not receive this data.

What stays on your phone

  • How many free conversation minutes are left (on iPhone and iPad in the Keychain, so it survives deleting the app; on Android in the app's private storage).
  • The two languages you chose last.
  • That you have read the first-run notice.
  • A small count of the app's own events (conversations started, lines fixed, transcripts shared), capped at 500 entries, with no caption text, audio or identifier. It is never sent anywhere.

Talktitles adds nothing to the phone's search index.

Permissions

  • Microphone and speech recognition: to hear the conversation and turn it into captions on the phone.
  • Notifications (Android): to show that Talktitles is listening, with a Stop button. It is asked for only when you start your first conversation.

Age signal

Where a law requires it, Talktitles reads the age range the App Store or Google Play shares at first launch, to apply the rules for younger users. The app does not send it anywhere.

Children

Talktitles is not directed to children, and we collect no data from anyone.

Deleting your data

There is no account and nothing on a server, so there is nothing for us to delete. Deleting the app removes everything it kept on the phone. On iPhone and iPad, the free-minutes count stays in the Keychain. Your purchase is kept by the App Store or Google Play and can be restored.

Changes and contact

If this ever changes, this page and both stores' privacy answers change first. Questions: support@8086.llc.
