What the other person says, in your language, as they say it.

Lay one phone flat between you. Each of you reads the other person's words as live, translated captions on your own half of the screen. Nobody holds a button and nobody passes the phone.

Talktitles is for two people in the same room, one phone lying between you, each reading the other's speech in your own language. It is not for phone calls, not for groups of three or more, and not for translating text or photos.

Free to download with 20 conversation minutes. Then one purchase, $9.99 in the U.S., no subscription.

Privacy in one line: no account, no ads, no analytics, and the conversation never leaves the phone.

Why it is worth paying once

  • It keeps listening

    Talktitles listens to both of you the whole time and works out who is speaking from the language they speak, so a quick back-and-forth lands on the right half. "Listening" shows on both halves whenever the microphone is on.

  • Fix a misheard line in two taps

    Tap a finished line and change what was heard. The translation updates on both halves and the line is marked "Fixed". A line on the wrong side moves over in one tap.

  • On the phone, offline

    Speech recognition and translation run on the phone. Download your two languages once, then it works in Airplane Mode. Your conversation is never uploaded. The phone uses the network only to buy or restore the unlock and to install a language pack (and, on Android, for the basic diagnostics Google's translation library sends).

Good to know

  • Captions are machine translations and can be wrong. Do not use them for medical, legal or emergency conversations.
  • If someone else is being captioned, tell them before you start.
  • Reading, fixing and sharing a transcript stay free. The only thing the purchase adds is unlimited conversation time.
  • Large caption type follows your phone's text size, and dark mode is built in.
